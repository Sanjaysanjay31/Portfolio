EventConnect

A smart, full-stack platform that connects event organizers and students in a simple, fast, and secure way. In many colleges, students struggle to discover events, and organizers struggle to manage registrations and participants — EventConnect solves both sides from one centralized place. WorkMithra

A voice-first mobile app that connects everyday clients with nearby skilled workers — plumbers, electricians, painters, and more — in their own language. Built for users who prefer speaking over typing, WorkMithra uses on-device speech, real-time translation, and an AI assistant to handle the entire booking flow: discover workers on a map, hear their details read aloud, and confirm the job with a simple voice command. End-to-end OTP auth, location-aware matching, and an LLM-driven intent layer make the experience feel natural even for first-time smartphone users.
